CRITICAL — DO NOT modify the first 11 lines of `netlify.toml`. The `[functions]` block is for the `netlify/functions/submit-quote/` Function from Plan 03-03 and is unrelated to the build-tool migration.

DO NOT move the `/materials` -> `/styles#materials` 301 redirect from `public/_redirects` into `netlify.toml`. Netlify processes `_redirects` BEFORE `netlify.toml` `[[redirects]]`, so the specific 301 in `_redirects` correctly wins over the catch-all `/*` rewrite.
