<objective>
Switch the Resend `from:` sender in both Netlify Functions to the dedicated transactional subdomain `mail.shapesmith.studio` (already added + DNS-verified in Resend by the owner).

Purpose: The apex `shapesmith.studio` is managed by Proton Mail for inbound (owner's primary inbox `jkrush@shapesmith.studio`). Proton's SPF record already claims authority over the apex, so adding Resend as a sender on the apex would require complex DNS surgery and would still hit same-domain self-send anti-spam checks. Using a dedicated subdomain isolates Resend's SPF/DKIM from Proton entirely.

Output: Two one-line edits + comment updates in two files, bundled onto the existing `fix/snipcart-webhook-resend-error-check` branch as a single atomic commit.
</objective>
